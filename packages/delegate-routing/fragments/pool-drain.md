Before the first delegate, invoke the absolute `delegate-routing-plan` command
rendered in this runtime's skill with the requested roles and caller-observed
`--launch-context`. Use its decision as canonical; do not reproduce Reader
discovery or pace arithmetic manually. The helper must run the Reader of every
registered pool listed in its output, including pools outside this runtime's
launch targets; Reader commands belong to registered pool keys, not separate
registrations per target runtime.

The helper ranks fitting measured pools by worst-window pace, using each
window's reported length; a monthly pool is paced against its month, not the
week. Failed or incomplete Readers and ambiguous account or window scope are
unmeasured with reasons: exclude them while fitting measured pools exist and
report that once per snapshot. With no measurable fitting pool, use fit alone
and report budget/session uncertainty. Never treat exhaustion as unmeasured.

The configured session pool drains last: select it only when every other fitting
measured pool is behind or level in pace and its minimum remaining share across
applicable windows is strictly more than a fifth. That fifth is the buffer for
the person using the session; unknown session identity cannot certify it. Aim
for every pool to reach its reset near empty. Do not report pool numbers unless
asked.

Tier and pace do not establish role fit, trial eligibility, live availability or
cross-maker independence. Supply that evidence in the launch context and honor
exclusions. For finder, first-check, second-check and judge, never step down a
tier; report a loop failure when no qualified strong/frontier route is
available. The helper launches no delegates; canonical launch lines are routing
specifications for the orchestrator to bind to native tools or named launchers.
