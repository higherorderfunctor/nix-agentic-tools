An adjudicator, separate from the worker, finder and refuter, rules on each
finding from the evidence against the rubric. A cheap adjudicator with the
written rubric is enough for checklist criteria; use one strong adjudicator for
taste or architecture. No role argues for its own output. A finding that does
not break the goal's happy path or a stated guarantee is reported as a gap, not
fixed. An unsettled finding goes back to the orchestrator to validate.
