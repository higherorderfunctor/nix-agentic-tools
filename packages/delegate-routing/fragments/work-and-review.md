Use when a delegate makes a change someone will act on: code, configuration or
prose.

Keep producers and evaluators apart, here and beyond this workflow. The delegate
that forms a change, plan, diagnosis or research finding is never its only
evaluator. Pair coder with reviewer, planner with executor, researcher with
assessor, and diagnostician with fixer. A delegate retains its own framing; an
independent evaluator checks that framing against the goal and evidence.
