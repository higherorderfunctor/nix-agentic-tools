Use when a delegate makes a change someone will act on: code, configuration or
prose, and also a plan, a diagnosis or a research finding. Keep the producer and
the evaluator separate: the delegate that forms an artifact is never its only
evaluator, or the only delegate that acts on it. Pair coder with reviewer,
planner with executor, researcher with assessor, and diagnostician with fixer. A
delegate that has committed to a framing keeps it downstream, so a fixer that
owns its diagnosis can redefine the failure to finish, for example by disabling
the failing check instead of fixing the cause.
